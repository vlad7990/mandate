# Member management

Admins run their own team from **Settings → Members**. Mandate's
founder opens the front door; everything after that belongs to the
organisation.

## Inviting a colleague

Invitations work exactly like the door you came through: the admin
names the person, picks their role, and receives a join link to hand
over. Nothing is emailed. The link is single-use, expires in fourteen
days, and whoever redeems it joins **active**, in the role chosen at
issue — the invite is the approval. Open invitations are listed and can
be revoked at any time; a revoked invitation cannot be reactivated.

## The five staff roles

- **Admin** — everything a manager can do, plus org settings, member
  roles and the skills studio.
- **Manager** — runs the desk: every recruiter's mandates, pipeline
  and fees, and reassigns leads. Cannot administer members or author
  skills.
- **Recruiter** — runs mandates end to end: sourcing, evaluation,
  shortlists, client exports and outreach.
- **Researcher** — sourcing and evaluation, with their own delivery
  objectives. Cannot open a mandate, publish a shortlist or contact a
  candidate.
- **Viewer** — read-only across the org, excluding fees and revenue.

The members screen carries the full capability-by-role matrix, so what
each role can touch is never a matter of memory. Client-side people
(hiring managers, HR) are invited through their own separate door and
never appear in this roster's role picker.

## Suspending and restoring

An admin can suspend any member — their session stops working within
one request, and nothing is deleted — and restore them later. Four
refusals are built in, stated in words when you hit them:

- Founder accounts are managed by Mandate and cannot be changed here.
- Agent principals are managed from the operator console, not the
  members screen.
- You cannot suspend your own account.
- The organisation's last active admin cannot be suspended — that
  would lock the organisation.

The same last-admin protection applies to role changes: an organisation
can never admin itself to zero.
