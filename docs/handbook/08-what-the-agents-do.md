# What the agents do — and never do

Every agent judgment in Mandate runs as a **principal**: its own
identity in the database, its own credential, its own kill switch, and
its own name in the activity trail. No agent acts as you, and the
editorial lines below are enforced in the database, not in a prompt.
The full registry — every agent, its status, what it does and what
stays human — is a page inside the product, at Agents.

## The working set

Agents cover the loop in phases: understanding the role (intake,
company intelligence, culture, calibration, role spec), discovering
people (boolean search, candidate search and research), evaluating them
(CV parsing, evaluation, ranking, triangulation, psychology, executive
intelligence), engaging them (outreach strategy, relationship,
engagement, pre-screen), delivering (positioning, shortlist, search
health, desk digest), and assisting you (the feedback interpreter and
Mandy, the copilot). Each registry entry states its one job and its
"stays human" line.

## The lines that hold

- **Decision support — never a recommendation.** Scores are weighted
  evidence against your approved calibration; no agent output tells
  you whom to hire, and the interfaces are built so a reading cannot
  be mistaken for a verdict. The pre-screen conversation goes
  further: no verdict, no score, no pass — anywhere, by construction.
- **No agent acts as you.** Every agent write lands under the agent's
  own name in the activity trail; your name is never borrowed.
- **No agent contacts anyone on its own.** Outreach drafts wait for a
  person to send them; portal links are handed over by hand.
- **Every agent can be stopped.** Each principal has an independent
  kill switch, and a suspended agent's work simply does not happen —
  the product says so rather than pretending.
- **You steer them in writing.** Agents read your Skills Studio
  instructions on every run; steering lives with your team, not in a
  support ticket.

## What the words on the screen mean

Where the product shows you example content before your own data
exists, it says so — sample screens are labelled as sample, and
marketing illustrations are labelled illustrative. Where an agent's
reading appears, the sentence beside it is the doctrine of this whole
handbook in six words: decision support, never a recommendation.
