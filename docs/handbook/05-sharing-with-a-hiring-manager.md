# Sharing with a hiring manager

A hiring manager never needs an account, a password, or a login. The
whole client-sharing contract is a **link**: the recruiter mints it,
hands it over, and can kill it at any moment.

## Minting a link

From a mandate's HM Portal tab you generate a link, optionally tied to
a named client contact and labelled — one labelled link per person, so
each can be revoked individually without disturbing the others. Naming
a contact does not create an account; externals stay on the token path.

- Links expire **thirty days** after issue.
- Revocation is immediate and permanent — a revoked link cannot be
  reactivated, only replaced.
- Nothing is emailed by the product. You copy the link and hand it
  over yourself.

## What the hiring manager sees

The link opens a read-only portal scoped to that one mandate: the role,
the search status, when it last moved, and the current slate —
each candidate with their fit reading, tier, rank and headline. The
fit figures are weighted against the approved calibration and presented
as decision support — never a recommendation; the slate is the search
team's to argue, not the software's. Before a slate exists the portal
says so honestly and shows the top-ranked candidates so far.

## Feedback flows back

Under each candidate the hiring manager can rate — Strong Yes, Yes,
Maybe, No — and write what stands out or concerns them, plus a
slate-level note. Submitting sends every rating and note into the
mandate's feedback record, where the Feedback Interpreter reads it for
preference changes and contradictions and the search team calibrates
against it.

A dead link tells the visitor to ask the recruiter for a fresh one, and
portal opens are recorded on the mandate's activity trail.
