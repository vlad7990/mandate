# The mandate loop

A search in Mandate runs as a loop: describe the role once, calibrate
what matters, feed it candidates, and let ranked evidence accumulate
until a slate is ready. Every agent output along the way is decision
support — never a recommendation; you review, edit and approve at each
step that matters.

## Intake — one line

A new mandate starts at **New mandate** with a single line — "Head of
IT Ops for RBC Capital Markets" is the whole input. On submit the
Intake, Company Intelligence, Role Spec and Calibration agents activate,
the mandate page opens immediately, and the title resolves from
"Analyzing…" to the parsed role and company within seconds.

## The onboarding wizard

Each mandate then carries a five-step questionnaire that turns your
knowledge of the client into calibration:

1. **Origin** — where this mandate came from and why now.
2. **Must-haves** — non-negotiables, applied as hard filters.
3. **Anti-patterns** — deal-breakers you never want to see again.
4. **Stakeholders** — the interview panel and what each person probes.
5. **Priorities** — the role's priority signals, each with a weight.

## Calibration

From the intake and the wizard, the Calibration Agent derives a scoring
model across five dimensions — technical, domain, leadership,
regulatory, transformation — each weighted 0–10 and shown with its
rationale on the mandate page. There is no slider to drag: the levers
are the wizard's answers, the Optimize screen, recalibration from
feedback, and Calibration History, where every prior model can be
inspected and restored.

## CV upload

Candidates enter as documents: PDF or DOCX, 10 MB max, dropped onto CV
Intake. The CV Parsing Agent extracts role history, scale, tech
exposure and archetype in one pass — usually 20 to 40 seconds — into
org-scoped private storage.

## Evaluation

Each candidate page holds the parsed profile (every field editable),
the evidence, and a fit reading weighted against the approved
calibration. The page says it itself, verbatim: decision support —
never a recommendation.

## Ranking and the shortlist

The ranking leaderboard scores every parsed candidate against the
calibration and sorts them into four tiers — Optimal, Strong, Stretch,
Below Bar. From there you compare candidates side by side, generate the
comparative market report (exportable as Markdown, HTML or PDF, with a
client email draft), and build the slate: three, five, or a custom
size, finalised with an explicit submission.

## Feedback and recalibration

Feedback — yours or a hiring manager's — is interpreted for preference
changes, contradictions and bias flags, and can recalibrate the scoring
model. The loop closes: the next ranking pass reflects what the client
actually said.

## The weekly report

Each mandate has a Weekly Report: an executive summary, top candidates,
and what was sourced this week, generated on demand and archived by
week. It exports as Markdown or PDF and can open a pre-filled client
email draft.
