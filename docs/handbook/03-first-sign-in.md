# First sign-in

Signing in lands you on the **Portfolio** — the authenticated home.
Everything in the product hangs off a left rail with four groups:

- **Workspace** — Portfolio, Desk (managers and admins), Analytics,
  Objectives, Placements, Activity.
- **Search** — Clients, Mandates, Candidates (with Pool search),
  Network.
- **Intelligence** — Executive Intelligence, with Competencies and
  Role templates.
- **System** — Agents, Skills studio, and Settings (Members appears
  for admins).

## The Portfolio

Once your searches are running, the Portfolio shows your action queue,
your tasks, four headline numbers — active mandates, candidates, added
this week, and at-risk mandates flagged by the Search Health Agent
(flagged for attention, never judged for you — decision support, never
a recommendation) — and a table of active mandates with their status
and health.

## Before you have data: sample mode

A brand-new workspace has nothing in it, so the Portfolio opens in
**sample mode**: an example account showing what Mandate looks like in
use. Sample content is always labelled — a banner reading "Sample
dashboard" states plainly that none of it is your data, and every
sample screen carries a `// sample data` marker in its meta line. The
moment your own first mandate exists, the real workspace takes over.

## The Desk

Admins and managers also see **Desk** — desk oversight across every
recruiter: load, pipeline, placements, open tasks, and mandate
reassignment, all derived from the live rows. In its own words: nothing
scored, nothing asserted.
