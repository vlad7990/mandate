# The candidate portal, and erasure

Candidates have a door of their own. From a candidate's page the
recruiter gets a portal link — one live link per person, thirty days,
reissuing returns the same one — and hands it over. Nothing is
emailed by the product.

## What a candidate sees

The portal shows the person exactly what is held about them and lets
them act on it:

- **What is held** — contact details, current title and company,
  profile links, whether a CV is on file, and how they were found.
- **The searches they are in** — each live search by role title and
  stage, with a **Withdraw** button per search. Withdrawing is visible
  to the search team, who stop considering them. The client behind a
  search stays confidential until the team introduces it.
- **Correct your details** — phone, location and profile links are
  editable in place; the identity anchor (name or LinkedIn) and email
  changes go through the search team.
- **Submit a newer CV** — uploads go to the team for review by hand; a
  new file never replaces the old one automatically.

## Asking for erasure

The portal's last section lets a candidate ask the firm to erase what
it holds about them, with an optional note. The contract is stated
where the button lives: the request is reviewed by a person, erasure is
not automatic, and the team may need to keep some records where the law
requires it.

Once filed, the request is visible to the search firm's admin — whose
screen says plainly to stop working the person — and to Mandate, where
a founder reviews it and either declines or resolves it, recording a
note of what was erased. Review and execution sit with a person at
every step.
