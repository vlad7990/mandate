# Requesting access

Mandate is in closed beta with a hand-picked group of executive search
firms. There is no self-serve signup: access is granted by approval, a
founder reads every application, and nothing asks for a credit card —
there is no trial tier to fall into.

## The form

The request lives at [/request-access](/request-access). Six fields, and
only the first two are required:

- **Full name** and **email** — required; the email is where your
  invitation will eventually arrive.
- Company, role or title, how you heard about us, and what you are
  trying to solve — all optional, all read by a person.

When the request lands you see **Request received** and a promise we
keep by hand: we reply within 48 hours, and if we approve, a sign-up
link arrives at the email you provided.

## What happens behind it

- Submissions are rate-limited. If the limiter refuses you it says so
  politely — "We've already received a request from you. We'll be in
  touch." — and your earlier request stands.
- Sending the same email twice does not create two requests, and the
  form will not tell a stranger whether an address has applied.
- Your request enters a review queue that only Mandate's founder can
  read. What happens next is the subject of the following chapter.
