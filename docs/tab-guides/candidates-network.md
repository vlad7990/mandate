# Network

Your candidates folded into **people** — one row per human, however many
mandates they have appeared in.

## What you do here

1. Read the rollups: by archetype, by domain, top by average score, most
   versatile.
2. Find people you have already assessed who fit something new.
3. Maintain the relationship record — state, follow-ups, and
   **do-not-contact**.

## What done looks like

Anyone you should not approach is marked do-not-contact, and the people
worth re-approaching have a follow-up date.

## What this screen will not do

It will not contact anyone on its own, and no agent may set or clear
do-not-contact — **that flag is only ever a human's**.

"Top by average score" is a mean across every appearance, not a ranking
of who is best: someone assessed once against an easy brief will outrank
someone assessed five times against hard ones. Read it as a shortlist of
who to look at again, not as a league table.

Where two records were merged into one person, the row states how, so a
wrong merge can be found.

## Where this leads

To the person's candidate record, or into a mandate you are staffing.
