# Candidates

Every candidate across every mandate, one screenful at a time.

## What you do here

1. Search and filter — by mandate, stage, tier or archetype.
2. Open a candidate to read the evaluation, the evidence behind it, and
   the second opinion.
3. Move someone through the pipeline as reality changes.
4. Upload a CV to add someone to a mandate.

## What done looks like

Every candidate has been read by a human, not just scored, and their
stage matches where they actually are.

## What this screen will not do

It will not decide who to submit. Three rules govern what you see, all
about honesty rather than styling:

- **Partial data is normal.** A CV still parsing, or a candidate found
  but not yet scored, appears inline with honest placeholders — hiding a
  row until it is complete would make the count wrong and an upload look
  lost.
- **Tier is a band, not a grade.** Tier 1 carries the accent; tiers 2–4
  stay neutral, and nothing is red. A tier-3 candidate is not a failure.
- **Dedupe is visible.** Where two records were merged into one person,
  the Network view states how — so a wrong merge can be found rather
  than silently trusted.

A CV upload takes roughly a minute and a half to parse, evaluate and be
second-opinioned. It is not stuck.

## Where this leads

To the candidate's own page, and on to the shortlist.
