# Desk

The manager's screen: how the work is distributed across the people
doing it. One row per recruiter, derived from rows that already exist.

## What you do here

1. Compare recruiters by **mandates led**, candidates in them, and
   placements owned.
2. Look at **last activity** to find a mandate nobody has touched.
3. Drill into any row to reach the underlying mandates or placements.

## What done looks like

You can name who is overloaded, who has capacity, and which mandate has
gone quiet — and you got there without asking anyone.

## What this screen will not do

Nothing here is scored, weighted or invented. **Every number is a count
you could reproduce by hand**, deliberately, because this is the screen
most at risk of contradicting its own drill-downs. It makes no judgement
about performance, and it will not tell you who is doing well — activity
counts are not quality.

Money stays behind `fees:read` on its own pages; the desk is a persona
boundary, not a money one.

## Where this leads

Into **Mandates** or **Placements** for the row you were reading.
