# Settings

Your own account, the organisation's identity, and the way into the
screens that configure how the desk behaves.

## What you do here

1. Change your own **name** and **password**. Changing a password leaves
   your other signed-in sessions active.
2. Admins: set **Advisory mode** — on, an evaluation leads with what to
   test at interview; off, it leads with the verdict. The verdict is
   never hidden either way.
3. Follow the links to the screens that do the configuring:
   **Members**, **Skills Studio**, **Models**, and for founders,
   **Platform ops**.
4. Read the rosters and, if any are open, the candidate **erasure
   requests** — stop working anyone listed there.

## What done looks like

Your account details are right, advisory mode matches how your clients
want to read an evaluation, and no erasure request is sitting unread.

## What this screen will not do

It will not approve a pending person, suspend or restore anyone, change
anyone's role, or edit the organisation's name or slug — those moved to
**Members** and, for founders, platform operations. It cannot change
your email address; ask Mandate.

It does not act on an erasure request. It shows you the request so you
stop working that person; Mandate reviews and executes it.

Links you lack the capability for are **not shown** rather than shown
and bounced — a link that bounces reads as a broken product rather than
a restricted one. **Invoice templates** is not linked from here; it is
reached from the Invoices screen.

## Where this leads

**Members** to change who can do what, **Skills studio** to steer the
agents, **Models** to choose which model answers.
