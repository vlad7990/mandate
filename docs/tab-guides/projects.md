# Mandates

Every search in the workspace, live and closed.

## What you do here

1. Start a **new mandate** from a single line — *"Head of IT Ops for RBC
   Capital Markets"* is the whole input. Intake, Company Intelligence,
   Role Spec and Calibration activate on submit.
2. Complete the mandate's five-step questionnaire — origin, must-haves,
   anti-patterns, stakeholders, priorities. **This is the step that
   decides how well everything downstream works**: calibration is
   derived from your answers.
3. Open a mandate to work it — upload CVs, review, rank, shortlist.

## What done looks like

The mandate has a final role spec, a calibration you agree with, and
candidates arriving against it.

## What this screen will not do

It will not fill a role for you. Every agent output on a mandate is
decision support, **never a recommendation** — you review, edit and
approve at each step that matters.

Three columns you see in the sample — candidate counts, tier-1 counts,
day-of-search — are not on the real table: they need a per-mandate
aggregate this page does not compute, and a column of dashes on every
row is worse than no column. The mandate's own page has them.

## Where this leads

Into the mandate, and from there to **Candidates**.
