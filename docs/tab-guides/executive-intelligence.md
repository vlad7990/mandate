# Executive Intelligence

The front door to the executive module: four counts, your five most
recent executive searches, and a map of which parts of the due-diligence
arc exist today.

## What you do here

1. Read the four counts — active searches, approved profiles, role
   templates, competencies — to see whether the module has anything
   running.
2. Start a **New Executive Search**, or **Start From Template** if a
   role template already describes the job.
3. Open a recent search to continue work already underway.

## What done looks like

You have left for a search. Nothing is saved on this screen — it has no
form and no action; it is a landing page.

## What this screen will not do

It runs no agent. The line it carries — *AI-generated decision support,
reviewed and approved by a person* — is a standing statement about the
module, not a verdict about anything on the page.

**Five tiles on the module map are deliberately dead**, greyed and
marked Phase 2: active interviews, risk reviews, final reports,
executive advisors, and module settings. They are drawn so you can see
the shape of the module, and they do nothing. Four of the live tiles —
role success profiles, candidates, interview plans, assessments — all
lead to the same list of searches; there is no separate screen behind
each one yet.

If you cannot create a search, **New Executive Search is simply absent**
rather than greyed out: it needs `mandates:write`, and a disabled button
is a promise the product cannot keep. Everything else here is readable
by every role.

## Where this leads

Into an executive search, or to **Role templates** to start from a
defined one.
