# Members

Who is on this desk, what each of them can do, and the only screen that
changes it.

## What you do here

1. Read the roster. The meta line says how many accounts and how many
   admins, and warns you when there is only one admin left.
2. Change someone's **Role**, then press **Apply**. It is deliberately
   not save-on-change — an arrow key through a select would otherwise
   demote whoever you scrolled past.
3. **Suspend** someone who should no longer have access; **Restore**
   them later. Nothing is deleted either way.
4. **Issue Invitation** for a new colleague — name, email, role — then
   hand over the link that appears.
5. If an admin grant is waiting, **Approve** or **Decline** another
   admin's proposal, or **Withdraw** your own.

## What done looks like

Everyone active holds the role their work actually needs, nobody who has
left still has a session, and no proposal is sitting unanswered.

## What this screen will not do

**Nothing is emailed.** The invitation is a link you hand over yourself.
It is single-use, expires in fourteen days, and whoever redeems it joins
**active** with the role you chose. A spent or expired link shows one
honest dead end — it does not say which kind of dead, on purpose.

**Making someone an admin takes two admins.** You propose it; a
different admin approves. Your own proposal shows no Approve button at
all rather than one that cannot work. The rule engages once there are
two active admins — below that, a grant lands immediately. Removing an
admin stays a one-person act.

It refuses to lock you out: you cannot suspend yourself, you cannot
suspend the last active admin, and founder and agent accounts carry no
controls here at all — agents are operated from platform operations.

There is no remove-a-member (suspend is the removal), no resend, no way
to see a previously-issued link again, no bulk action, no search, and no
client-side roles — those arrive by invitation from a client screen.
Approving a **pending** account is a founder act elsewhere; pending
people appear here, as viewers, until then.

This screen needs `org:manage`, which only an admin holds.

## Where this leads

Back to **Settings**. New accounts join as viewer — promote them here.
