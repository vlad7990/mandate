# Analytics

The shape of the workspace over time: where candidates sit, how mandates
are faring, and how fast work is arriving.

## What you do here

1. Read **Candidates by pipeline stage** to find where people pile up —
   a bulge at one stage is the bottleneck.
2. Check **Projects by health status** for mandates drifting.
3. Use **Weekly velocity** (candidates added per week, last 8 weeks) to
   see whether sourcing is keeping pace.
4. Read **Judgment calibration** — how the system's verdicts compare
   with what actually happened to those candidates.

## What done looks like

You can say where the pipeline is stuck and whether it is getting better
or worse.

## What this screen will not do

It will not diagnose the cause of a bottleneck, and it does not forecast.
Judgment calibration only becomes meaningful once real outcomes have
accumulated — with few placements behind it, treat it as early signal,
not as a verdict on the system's accuracy.

## Where this leads

To the stage that is blocked — usually **Candidates** filtered by that
stage, or the mandate whose health slipped.
