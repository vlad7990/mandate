# Clients

The companies you recruit for, and every mandate run against each.

## What you do here

1. Add a client, or open one to read its record.
2. See the mandates run for that company, and how they went.
3. Keep contacts current — they are who invoices and portal invitations
   reach.

## What done looks like

Every live mandate has a client record behind it with a contact you
could actually email.

## What this screen will not do

It will not research a company for you — that is the Company
Intelligence agent, on the mandate. Resolving and linking a client is
always **the recruiter's act**, never an agent's: the system will not
decide that two similar company names are the same organisation.

The list is not paginated, deliberately: an agency works for tens of
companies, not tens of thousands.

## Where this leads

To a **Mandate** for that client, or to **Invoices** when there is
something to bill.
